# Open Radio App Cloud Account Deletion Guide

## Introduction

This guide is for users of the Open Radio app who wish to delete their cloud account. A cloud account in Open Radio allows you to save your favorite stations and access them across different devices. If you no longer need this feature or if you're concerned about your privacy, you might want to delete your cloud account.

Open Radio uses the Firebase auth library to manage Cloud Accounts. You can read more about it [here](https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/).

## Steps to Request Cloud Account Deletion

1. Open the Open Radio app on your device.
2. Navigate to the 'Settings' menu.
3. Within the 'Settings' menu, find the 'Cloud Storage' section.
4. In the 'Cloud Storage' section, look for a button labeled with 'Delete' icon.
5. Tap on the 'Delete Account' button.
6. A prompt will appear, informing you of the consequences of deleting your account. If you agree, proceed with the deletion.

## Data Retention

Upon deletion request, the following data associated with your account will be deleted:

- User Data: Your locally added and favorite stations.

Please note that the deletion is immediate and irreversible. Once your account is deleted, all associated data is removed and cannot be recovered.
